The Sacred Art

About Our Academy

Aarudra Natiyalaya is a traditional Bharatanatyam academy focused on teaching classical dance with discipline, expression, rhythm, devotion and cultural values.

Originating in the temples of Tamil Nadu over two thousand years ago, Bharatanatyam is a divine union of Bhava (expression), Raga (melody), Tala (rhythm), and Natya (drama). At our academy, every student learns not only the technique but the heritage, devotion, and grace behind each movement — carrying forward a timeless parampara.

Our Guru

Guru Sureka Manoj Kumar

Founder & Artistic Director · Aarudra Natiyalaya

A devoted exponent of Bharatanatyam, Guru Sureka Manoj Kumar has dedicated her life to teaching classical dance rooted in tradition, discipline, and devotion.

With a deep commitment to the authentic values of the art, she nurtures each student's technique, expression, and cultural understanding — guiding them patiently from their very first Adavu to a complete stage performance. Her teaching blends classical rigour with warmth, ensuring this sacred temple art thrives in every student's heart.

“When the body speaks and the soul listens, that is when dance becomes divine.”
